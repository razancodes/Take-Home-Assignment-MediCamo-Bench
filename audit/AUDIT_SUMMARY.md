# Comprehensive Data Audit Summary

This document synthesizes the findings generated by our automated rule-based checks and the LLM-assisted triage (`audit_notes.csv`). By programmatically scanning the records, we uncovered severe structural and semantic flaws in the underlying patient data that immediately invalidate taking structured fields at face value. 

## 1. Structural Failures (System-Wide)

Our `checks.py` suite scanned the entire dataset for structural integrity and uncovered systemic parsing failures upstream of our benchmark:

- **The Broken Negation Flag (`is_negated_never_true`)**: 
  We audited all 406 diagnosis entries across the dataset. The `is_negated` boolean flag evaluates to `False` for **every single entry**. This means the upstream parser completely failed to capture negated clinical concepts, polluting the structured data with false positives.
- **Silent Medication Parsing Failures (`null_structured_with_raw`)**: 
  Dozens of medications (e.g., *inj erypeg 50mcg*, *sugary dm 100/10/500*, *ab phyllin sr tablet*) have a raw `rx_med_name` but their parsed `medicine_name`, `generic_name`, and `medicine_status` are explicitly `null`. Relying on the structured `generic_name` for medication reconciliation will result in massive recall failures.

## 2. Selected Clinical Contradictions

By comparing the raw free-text fields with the structured arrays, we discovered direct contradictions where the structured data dangerously hallucinates or misrepresents the clinician's notes.

### Case 1: The Neuropathy Contradiction (Patient P0010, V00147)
- **Raw Text (`raw_diagnosis`)**: Explicitly states `"no neuropathy"`.
- **Structured Data (`diagnoses`)**: Lists `"neuropathy"`. Due to the systemic broken negation flag mentioned above, `is_negated` is incorrectly set to `false`. 
- **Impact**: An LLM blindly trusting the structured data will wrongly conclude the patient suffers from neuropathy.

### Case 2: The Ghost Complaints (Patient P0010, V00148)
- **Raw Text (`raw_complaints`)**: Explicitly states `"no headache reeling"`.
- **Structured Data (`complaints`)**: Uncritically lists `"headache"` as an active symptom. 
- **Impact**: The parser stripped the negation and populated a positive symptom. 

### Case 3: The Gynaecological Male (Patient P0006, V00073)
- **Patient Profile**: Demographics clearly state `gender: "m"`.
- **Structured Data (`medications`)**: The medication *haemaday tablet* is incorrectly mapped in the metadata to `therapy: "gynaec"` and `category: "gynaec_haematinics,iron+comb"`. 
- **Impact**: (Flagged by Gemma-4-31B-it triage). This is a critical mapping error that could trigger false demographic/medication safety warnings if an LLM is analyzing appropriateness of therapy.

## 3. Hallucination Bait (Temporal and Status Traps)

We identified several "traps" in the data that are perfect for testing an LLM's resistance to hallucination:

- **Massive HbA1c Gaps (`hba1c_gap`)**: 
  We identified huge blind spots in longitudinal care. For example, Patient P0006 has a **604-day gap** between HbA1c readings (Sep 2018 to May 2020). Patient P0010 has a **472-day gap** (Apr 2022 to Jul 2023). Asking an LLM to evaluate glycemic control in these blind windows tests whether it will interpolate/invent values.
- **Medication "Entry and Exit" Status (`med_status_trap`)**: 
  Dozens of medications across all patients (e.g., *calpol 650mg*, *dolo 650mg*, *zentel 400 mg*) have a `medicine_status` of `"entry and exit"`, meaning they were started and discontinued on the exact same day. If an LLM is asked for "current active medications" and fails to check this status, it will incorrectly list acute, discontinued meds as chronic therapies. 
- **Diagnosis-Medication Incoherence (`dx_med_coherence`)**: 
  At numerous visits (e.g., P0006 V00075, P0003 V00040), the raw diagnosis explicitly mentions "hypertension" or "diabetes", but no recognized anti-hypertensive or anti-diabetic medication is found in the structured arrays for that visit.

## Conclusion

This audit unequivocally proves that naive data extraction on this dataset is dangerous. A robust clinical LLM must be capable of navigating these contradictions, identifying the systemic parsing failures (like broken negations), and refusing to hallucinate when asked to evaluate the massive temporal gaps.
